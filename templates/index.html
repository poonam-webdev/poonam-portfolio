01About

I'm a Graduate Computer Science Engineering woman who has spent most of my internships inside government and public-sector organisations — the Ministry of Electronics & IT, Indian Railways, Power Finance Corporation, and the Indian Chamber of Food and Agriculture.

That's given me a particular taste in engineering: I like systems where a login flow or a database schema has to survive an audit, not just a demo. I work full-stack — Python/Flask and Java/Spring on the backend, MongoDB and PostgreSQL for data — and I'm building out machine learning fundamentals alongside it.

This site itself runs on that stack: Flask serves the pages, MongoDB stores the content you're reading, and plain HTML/CSS/JS handles everything in the browser.
